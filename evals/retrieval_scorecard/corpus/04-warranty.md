chunk_id: warranty-window

The warranty lasts 18 months from shipment or 12 months from startup, whichever date comes first. How long the warranty lasts from shipment and from startup is those two clocks, both printed on the packing list.

chunk_id: warranty-exclude

Seal damage from a dry run is not covered by warranty. Chemical attack outside the published list is not covered either. Early weep in the first hour is not seal damage from a dry run.

chunk_id: warranty-claim

A warranty claim must include the serial number, the hour-meter reading, and photos of the gland. A claim missing the hour-meter reading is returned. Include those three items.

chunk_id: warranty-kit

Only seal kit HK-17 keeps the CP-440 seal warranty valid. A substitute kit voids the seal portion. HK-17 is the stores number on the packing list.
