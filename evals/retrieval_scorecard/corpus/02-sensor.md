chunk_id: sensor-zero

The zero current for the PS-9 pressure transmitter is 4ma with the vent open to atmosphere. A zero current that drifts needs a new zero. Do not copy the span reading into the zero.

chunk_id: sensor-span

The span current for the PS-9 at the reference pressure of 10bar is 20ma. The span screw is under the side cap. Leave the zero screw alone while setting span.

chunk_id: sensor-interval

Calibrate the PS-9 pressure transmitter every 180 days, and again after any overpressure trip. The 180-day interval is counted from the sticker date. How often it is calibrated is that 180-day interval.

chunk_id: sensor-line

Never calibrate the PS-9 while the process line is pressurized. Vent the line to atmosphere before a calibrator is connected. Calibration during pressure is not allowed.
