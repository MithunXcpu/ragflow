chunk_id: loto-energy

Lockout on the skid covers four energy sources: electrical, hydraulic, pneumatic, and stored spring. Each energy source gets its own isolation point. Which energy sources lockout covers is that list.

chunk_id: loto-personal

Two technicians may not share a single lock. Each worker applies a personal lock. A borrowed lock is not a personal lock, so two technicians do not share one.

chunk_id: loto-verify

After the locks are applied, the required check is a try-start. A try-start that does not move the machine passes. Clear the controls when the try-start is finished.

chunk_id: loto-group

A group lockbox is required when more than one craft works on the same isolation. The lead places the key in the lockbox. Personal locks go on the lockbox hasp.
