chunk_id: pump-torque

The tight setting for the CP-440 gland bolts on the process pump is 25 nm in a cross pattern. How tight the gland bolts should be is that 25 nm cross pattern after the seal faces are wet.

chunk_id: pump-steps

The seal replacement sequence before final tightening is: isolate the pump, drain the casing, pull the bearing frame, and seat the new seal on the shaft sleeve. Hand-fit the gland before any final tightening.

chunk_id: pump-tools

Bring a torque wrench, a seal hook, and solvent wipes for a gland job. Clean the studs before they go back on. The wrench is the tool, not the setting.

chunk_id: pump-symptoms

A failed CP-440 seal weeps at the gland. Bearing noise is a different fault and is not a seal symptom. Brown residue at the weep hole means the seal faces are worn.
